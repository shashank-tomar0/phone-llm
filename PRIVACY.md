# PhoneLM Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-13

PhoneLM is a local-first AI chat client. It is designed so that your
conversations stay on your device.

## What the app does NOT do

- We do not collect, store, or transmit your chats, prompts, images, or any
  personal data to us.
- There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any
  kind in the app.
- Chats, settings, and downloaded AI models are stored only on your device
  (local Hive storage and app-private storage).

## Data that leaves your device only when you choose it

- **Cloud AI providers (optional):** If you enable a cloud provider
  (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, Kimi) and enter your own API key, your
  prompts and conversation content are sent directly from your device to that
  provider. These requests go only to the provider you selected, using your
  key, and are governed by that provider's privacy policy. PhoneLM's developer
  never sees this data.
- **Cloud image generation (optional):** Same as above for the provider you
  configure.
- **Device cluster (optional):** If you use the phone-cluster feature, model
  inference data is exchanged between the phones on your own local Wi-Fi
  network. It never reaches any server on the internet.

## Network access the app itself uses

- Downloading AI model files from Hugging Face (public model files, no
  personal data sent).
- Checking GitHub Releases for app updates (the app fetches public release
  metadata; for installs from Google Play, updates come from Google Play and
  the in-app update check is disabled).

## Children

PhoneLM is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
any data from anyone.
